We do not ask for, and you should not send us, payment card numbers, bank details, passwords, Aadhaar or any other government identity number over WhatsApp.

Why we are allowed to use it

We use your information to answer the enquiry you started and to carry out the work you ask us to do. We send promotional messages only to people who have opted in, and every such message can be stopped — see below.

Who else sees it

  • Meta (WhatsApp) carries the messages. Their handling is covered by the WhatsApp Privacy Policy.
  • Our hosting provider runs the server and database that store the conversation.
  • Our own team, to answer you and complete your order.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it for anyone else's advertising.

How long we keep it

We keep your conversation and order history for as long as you are in touch with us about work, and afterwards only as long as we need it for our accounts and tax records. You can ask us to delete it sooner at any time.

Stopping messages

Reply STOP to any message and we will not send you promotional messages again. You can also block the number in WhatsApp.

Your choices

  • Ask what we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct it.
  • Ask us to delete it — see Data deletion.

Write to the address below and we will respond within 30 days.

Security

Access to the system is restricted to our team. Credentials and access keys held by the system are stored encrypted. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep sensitive personal information out of WhatsApp messages.

Children

This service is for business enquiries and is not directed at children under 18.
