Harmohinder Singh & Company (HS Company), Jaipur

If you have messaged our WhatsApp business number and want everything we hold about you removed, you can ask us and we will do it. There is no form to fill in and no charge.

How to ask

  1. Email hscompanyjaipur@gmail.com with the subject Delete my WhatsApp data, or send us a WhatsApp message saying DELETE MY DATA.
  2. Tell us the WhatsApp number you messaged us from, so we can find your record.
  3. We may send one message to that number to confirm the request really came from you.
What gets deleted
Your phone number, your name as WhatsApp showed it, the whole conversation including any photos and files you sent, the enquiry details the bot recorded, your lead notes, and your WhatsApp call records with us.

How long it takes

Within 30 days of us confirming the request. We will email you when it is done.

What we may have to keep

If you placed an order, Indian tax and accounting law requires us to keep the invoice and payment record for the statutory period. That record is kept only for that purpose and is not used to message you. Everything else is deleted.

A note about WhatsApp itself

Deleting your data from our system does not delete the chat from your own phone, and it does not remove anything held by Meta. To clear the chat on your side, delete it in WhatsApp. Meta's own handling is covered by the WhatsApp Privacy Policy.

Stopping messages without deleting

If you only want the promotional messages to stop, reply STOP to any message. Your enquiry history stays, but we will not send you campaigns again.
